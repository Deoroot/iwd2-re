# Open the Bag of Holding from the inventory item requester. That is the
# merchant-free route into CScreenStore: CScreenInventory::OnUseButtonClick
# case 3 hands the container's resref to CScreenWorld::StartStore.
# Requester panel 5: control 9 is "Open Container", control 4 is "Done".
#
# Needs the save that carries the container -- the default combat slot does not:
#   scripts/vm.sh smoke --save "000000039-store" 120 \
#       --ui scripts/scenarios/store-open-bag.txt --hit CScreenWorld::StartStore
# There the container sits in the first backpack slot, control 30 of panel 2.

sleep 60
expect control 5 9
click 5 9
