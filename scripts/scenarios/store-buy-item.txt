# Open the container store, put a party item into it, then buy it back. That
# drives CScreenStore::OnBuyItemButtonClick, which for a bag store with a bag
# open runs BuyBagItemsExt, BuyPersonalItemsExt and BuyBagItems in turn.
#
# The bag starts empty, so the sell leg is what gives the buy leg something to
# work on. Buy/sell is panel 2: controls 5..10 are the store (bag) rows, 13..18
# the group (party) rows, control 2 is Buy and control 3 is Sell.
#   scripts/vm.sh smoke --save "000000039-store" 200 \
#       --ui scripts/scenarios/store-buy-item.txt \
#       --hit CScreenStore::OnBuyItemButtonClick
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
goto inventory
sleep 120
expect screen inventory
rclick 2 30
sleep 60
click 5 9
sleep 240
expect screen store
click 2 13
sleep 90
click 2 3
sleep 180
