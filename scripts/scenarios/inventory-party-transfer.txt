# Prove giving an item to another party member still works.
#
# The transfer is not a drag: picking an item up parks it in
# CScreenInventory::m_pTempItem, and the next click decides where it goes.
# Clicking a portrait with something held runs
# CUIControlPortraitInventory::OnLButtonClick -> BeginSwap / SwapWithPortrait /
# EndSwap, which is the whole party-transfer path.
#
# The inventory screen's six portraits are panel 1, controls 0..5 (46x46 in a
# row); the backpack grid is panel 2. Which grid slots hold an item depends on
# the save, so this tries four of them in turn -- one pickup is enough for the
# gate, and a click on an empty slot is a no-op.
#
# Gate with --hit CScreenInventory::SwapWithPortrait.
#
# waitgo first: vm.sh only attaches the crash guard after the save loads.
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
goto inventory
wait inventory 600
sleep 40
click 2 5
sleep 15
click 1 1
sleep 20
click 2 6
sleep 15
click 1 1
sleep 20
click 2 15
sleep 15
click 1 1
sleep 20
click 2 16
sleep 15
click 1 1
sleep 20
