# Prove the state 0x7B feat-point picker builds and opens.
#
# Clicking Power Attack (or Expertise) in the innate picker does not fire it:
# the innate arm of OnLButtonPressed stashes the ability in
# m_currentAbilityResRef and enters state 0x7B, whose list comes from
# CInfButtonArray::BuildFeatPointsPickerList -> CGameSprite::GetFeatPointsButtonList.
#
# Route (slot N of the action bar is control (N + 6) on panel 1):
#   portrait 1 is a melee character with Power Attack
#   -> its world bar ends with Special Abilities 0x0A in slot 11, control 17
#   -> the innate picker (0x6A) holds Power Attack in slot 1, control 7
#
# The picker that opens has exactly six live cells: "Off" plus one per point,
# because the base attack bonus is capped at MAX_FEAT_POINTS.
#
# waitgo first: vm.sh only attaches the crash guard after the save loads.
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
click 1 1
sleep 30
bar
click 1 17
sleep 30
bar
click 1 7
sleep 30
bar
