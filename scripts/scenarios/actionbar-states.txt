# Reach a SECOND action-bar state, on our side, to match what
# `frida_orig.py --click 1:0` reaches on the original.
#
# The Prologue autosave opens on the GROUP bar (state 0x6E).  Clicking a party
# portrait selects that one character and the bar becomes the single-PC bar
# (state 0x72), which is nine more arms of CInfButtonArray::UpdateButtons'
# switch: the two weapon sets, Attack, Skills, the quick spells, the quick
# items, the quick abilities and Special Abilities.
#
# Portraits are panel 1, controls 0..5; the action bar is the same panel,
# controls 6..17.  So `click 1 0` is the first portrait.
#
# Pair with the armed Iwd2DebugLog in RenderButton (the snippet lives in
# scripts/probes/actionbar_settings.py): it fires on every change of
# (m_nState, m_nSelectedButton), so one run writes call=1 for 0x6E and call=2
# for 0x72, and --call picks between them.
#
# waitgo first: the crash guard only attaches once the save reports loaded.
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
click 1 0
sleep 60
