# Prove a modal feat toggles from the innate picker.
#
# Arterial Strike, Hamstring and Rapid Shot have no picker of their own: the
# innate arm of OnLButtonPressed flips the feat rank with
# CGameSprite::SetFeatRank and posts a CMessageAddEffect for the matching
# ICEWIND_CGAMEEFFECT_FEAT* effect, then pops back to the world bar.
#
# Route (slot N of the action bar is control (N + 6) on panel 1):
#   portrait 1 is a melee character; its world bar ends with Special
#   Abilities 0x0A in slot 11, control 17
#   -> its innate picker (0x6A) has two live cells, slot 1 and slot 2
#   -> slot 1 is Power Attack, which opens 0x7B (see feat-points-picker.txt)
#   -> slot 2 is a toggle, control 8
#
# Gate this with --hit CGameSprite::SetFeatRank: that call only happens on the
# toggle path, and the bar returning to 0x72 shows the click was consumed.
#
# waitgo first: vm.sh only attaches the crash guard after the save loads.
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
click 1 1
sleep 30
click 1 17
sleep 30
bar
click 1 8
sleep 30
bar
