# Exercise the spellbook picker's page arrows.
#
# Reaching a picker with more than 12 entries puts the bar in the paging
# layout -- 21 15 16 ... 1E 22 -- where slot 0 is page-up and slot 11 is
# page-down.  Clicking those moves m_nListStartIndex by ten entries, and
# UpdateButtons re-reads the list from FindIndex(m_nListStartIndex).
#
# Route to the picker (measured with `bar`, see quick-slot-picker.txt):
#   portrait 4 is a caster; control 11 is quick spell 0x48
#   -> rclick opens the customise menu 0x75, whose "Cast Spell" 0x24 is control 7
#   -> rclick that opens the spellbook picker 0x66, which pages.
#
# Slot N of the action bar is control (N + 6) on panel 1, so page-up is
# control 6 and page-down is control 17.
#
# waitgo first: vm.sh only attaches the crash guard after the save loads.
wait world 3000
waitgo 6000
sleep 30
click 1 4
sleep 30
rclick 1 11
sleep 30
rclick 1 7
sleep 30
bar
click 1 17
sleep 30
bar
click 1 6
sleep 30
bar
